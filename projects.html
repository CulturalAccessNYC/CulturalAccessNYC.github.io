Our Research

Data for evidence-based advocacy and field-building.

Cultural Accessibility NYC was conceived as a multi-phase initiative to assess needs, build partnerships, identify solutions, and take action to address gaps in the cultural accessibility infrastructure of the city. Initial funding from 2024 - 2026 has supported a research project to deliver baseline findings on New York City's cultural accessibility infrastructure. A public report, to be released in fall 2026, will summarize findings and outline strategic interventions to advance equitable access for disabled artists, cultural workers, and audiences.

This research includes both quantitative and qualitative research methods, including a survey open to cultural accessibility employees of NYC-based arts and culture organizations, a literature review, landscape analysis, and focus groups with Deaf and Disabled artists, accessibility specialists, advocates, and cultural professionals.

This research is being conducted by Turnstile Studio with support from Quantitative Research Consultant Chloe Adkins and oversight from Cultural Accessibility NYC’s steering committee and advisors.
