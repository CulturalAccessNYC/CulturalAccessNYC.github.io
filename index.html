Cultural Accessibility NYC

Cultural Accessibility NYC (interim group title) embodies a collaborative research and planning process toward a sustainable public/private resource that seeks to address systemic barriers to disability equity and accessibility across New York City's arts and culture sector. We aim to make critical interventions in the arts and cultural landscapes that will materially enhance the experience of New Yorkers and sustain the city's reputation as a global arts space.

CANYC brings together disabled artists, cultural leaders, and advocates to assess existing accessibility gaps and identify sustainable, actionable strategies that address systemic barriers and aggregate resources in the city’s cultural infrastructure (e.g. funding, policy, education, information) with a focus on advancing disability equity across New York City’s arts and culture sector.

CANYC grew out of the work of the Disability Justice & Accessibility Working Group and the Collaborative Fundraising Working Group of Culture@3. CANYC is fiscally sponsored by IndieSpace and supported by the Ford Foundation, the New York Community Trust, and the Blanche T. Enders Charitable Trust.
