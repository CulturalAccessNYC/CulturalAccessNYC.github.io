Cultural Accessibility NYC

Cultural Accessibility NYC (interim group title) is a collaborative research and planning initiative addressing systemic barriers to disability equity across New York City's arts and culture sector. Emerging from the Culture @ 3 Disability Justice and Accessibility Working Groups, it brings together disabled artists, advocates, and cultural leaders to assess gaps in accessibility and develop actionable, sustainable solutions.

Through research and community-driven processes, the initiative clarifies the distinctions between compliance, functional access, and true experiential equity. Current areas of focus include education and resources, workforce development, access to equipment and services, and infrastructure improvements — all grounded in collaborative, evidence-based strategies.

The goal is to make meaningful interventions in NYC’s cultural landscape that materially improve experiences for disabled artists, workers, and audiences — and sustain the city’s role as a global arts leader.

Education & Training

Building disability literacy and accessibility competency across arts organizations — from front-of-house staff to executive leadership.

Workforce Development

Expanding pathways for disabled artists, administrators, and technicians to build careers in NYC's cultural institutions.

Equipment & Services

Connecting organizations with assistive technology, interpretation services, captioning, and access tools they need to serve all audiences.
